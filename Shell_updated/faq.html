FAQ

What is shopcentralapac.com?

shop.shell.com is the only Shell approved source for branded merchandise in the United States (other regions to follow in 2023.) All items available on the site have been approved by our Brand Strategy team, support our ESG goals and are offered to Shell at the most competitive pricing.

Who is adm? What is their role?

adm, part of the global agency, adm Group, supports shop.shell.com as our approved supplier responsible for managing and procuring all global branded merchandise for Shell. The site is available for U.S. employees and any U.S. based approved Shell creative agency working on our behalf.

I can't find the items I need on shopcentralapac.com, can I place a custom order?

While every item on shop.shell.com has been pre-approved, and it may be a faster path to completing your order, you may not find exactly what you're looking for. Our Create a Project tool [START A PROJECT BRIEFING TOOL] will allow you to create a brief, which will immediately engage our Customer Support team. By simply filling out the form, you will receive custom product ideations that will meet the needs of your program. Please note, ALL items produced must comply with Shell's Global Brand Guidelines and oversight.

How do I order a few items and get them quickly?

Under the navigation header you will find both “Quick Ship” and “Low MOQ” options. There are dozens of Quick Ship items that will ship within 10 business days, while Minimum Order Quantity items will allow you to order a minimum less than 25 units.

Are Shell premiums impacted by the current global supply challenges?

Yes. Due to supply chain shortages and unprecedented demand on carriers, production and shipping times may be longer than expected. When planning an event, it is important to allow a minimum of 4-6 weeks to plan accordingly. This will also enable us to navigate around the current issues, while procuring the best merchandise at the most competitive price.

How can we ensure products are sustainable and support our ESG goals?

We are pleased to offer Shell a curated collection of sustainable merchandise. Items are highlighted by a special (leaf) icon and meet one or more of the following four criteria.

  1. Behavioural Change - Items that replace single use / disposable items and cause a behavioural change.
  2. Made from Recycled Materials - Items that are made from recycled plastic bottles (RPET), cotton, paper, metal and more.
  3. Sustainably Sourced Materials - Items made from plant-able paper, organic cotton or manufactured by factories that have clear messaging on their supply sources and commitments to sustainable farming / sourcing / production.
  4. Give Back - Items from suppliers that are members of 1% For The Planet, are B Corp Certified or suppliers that give back to underserved communities.

How does shopcentralapac.com calculate shipping charges?

The dimensional weight of the package and the distance from the sender address to the final delivery destination will impact the total cost of shipping. Please note that we use a variety of specialized vendors to produce branded merchandise assortments; therefore, items within your order could be produced by different vendors and result in multiple shipments.

How can I track my orders?

All orders placed through shop.shell.com can be viewed under your account. You can search using the different orders status options or by using the Order ID that is received at the time of placing your order.
